FAQ

What is an AI visibility report?
An AI visibility report shows which brands AI mentioned under given questions. Anscite produces these reports by regularly probing multiple AI engines with the same set of questions.
How does Anscite measure AI visibility?
Anscite regularly probes multiple AI engines with the same set of questions and produces reports. The reports show which brands AI mentioned under those questions.
Can I try Anscite before committing?
Yes, Anscite offers a Free First-round Probe to see how AI currently answers.
What is included in the managed service?
In the managed service (代运营), the client provides brand materials and Anscite handles the rest.
What is the self-service account?
The self-service account (自助账号) is for clients with their own content teams. It supports multiple brands in parallel, each with its own site and fact library.
What roles are available in the self-service account?
The self-service account has four role levels: super admin, admin, member, and read-only. The Read-only Role can view all reports and content but is blocked from any write operations.
